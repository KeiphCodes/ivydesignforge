Frausto's Street Tacos

A family-run Mexican food truck parked in one spot with regular weekday hours. Street tacos, burros, tortas and Sonoran hot dogs, plus catering for events.

Where the truck parks

1910 N La Cañada Dr #4, Green Valley, next to Talk of Town Salon. The truck stays put in a small shopping center, so you'll find it in the same spot every weekday.

Restaurantji's review summary mentions outdoor seating, ample parking and food brought out to your car on takeout orders.
